An account is required to start, and that is the only part that needs a connection. It is what keeps your progress safe if you lose your phone, what places you on the leaderboard, and what lets the game verify the date so daily quests cannot be farmed by changing your clock. After that first sign-in Solo Gainz runs offline — train, fight, open chests — and syncs everything the next time you have signal.
