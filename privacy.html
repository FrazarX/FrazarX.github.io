1. Our approach

Solo Gainz requires an account, and collects the minimum needed to run one. Play itself happens on your device: once you have signed in the App works with no connection at all, and your workouts, quests and combat are processed locally. What leaves your device is your game save and a small profile, which exist so your progress survives a lost phone, so you can appear on the leaderboard, and so the game can tell what day it actually is.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not run advertising or build advertising profiles.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is created and kept locally by the App. Some of it is also included in the cloud save described in section 3; the rest never leaves your device:

  • Progress data: XP, level, rank, coins, quests, chests, inventory, achievements, and lifetime workout statistics.
  • Activity data used for quests, such as step counts and the distance/pace of a run in progress, processed on-device.
  • Camera frames used to verify a weekly quest. These are analysed on your device in real time to count repetitions and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • App preferences: notification settings, reminder time, language, sound, and interface options. These stay on the device.

3. Your account and cloud save

An account is required to use Solo Gainz. Creating one and keeping it working involves:

  • Your email address, used to identify the account and to sign you in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile identifier from Google; we do not receive your Google password.
  • A player name of your choosing, which is public — it is how you appear on the leaderboard to other players.
  • Your date of birth, gender, experience level and fitness goal, collected during onboarding and used to scale your training targets. Date of birth is also how we apply our minimum age (section 11).
  • Your game save — the progress data in section 2 — stored so it survives losing or changing your phone.
  • Basic device information (model and operating-system version), used for support and to diagnose crashes.

This data is stored on our behalf by Supabase, which provides our hosted database and authentication. Access is restricted so that your account can only read and write its own save.

What is public: only your player name, level and rank tier, and only on the in-game leaderboard. Your email address, date of birth, gender and save contents are never shown to other players.

Why the date matters: daily quests reset once a day, so the App checks the current time against our server rather than trusting your phone's clock. This prevents progress being farmed by changing the device date. It records nothing about you.

Deleting the App from your device removes the local copy. To delete the cloud copy and your account, see section 12.

4. What we do not collect

Solo Gainz does not collect your phone number or contacts, record or upload camera footage, upload your location history or photo library, use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, build advertising profiles, or sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

Location is used only while a run is in progress, to measure distance and pace, and the route is not uploaded.

5. Device permissions and why they are used

The App requests each permission only in connection with a specific feature, and uses it strictly for that feature:

  • Location (GPS) — used only while you actively run a distance-tracking quest, to measure distance and speed on your device in real time. It is processed on-device to update quest progress and is not stored as a location history and not uploaded anywhere.
  • Physical activity / sensors — used, where available, to count steps and detect movement for fitness quests, processed on-device.
  • Notifications — used to send local reminders (a daily reminder at your chosen time, a midnight quest-refresh alert, and nudges to finish unfinished quests), generated on your device.
  • Camera & Photos / Media — used only if you choose to set a profile picture; the selected image stays on your device.
  • Local network / Wi‑Fi — used only for local (same-network) PvP discovery and matches, to find nearby devices for gameplay; it is not used to track you.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during interactions.

You can grant or revoke any permission at any time in your device's system settings. Some features may not work correctly without their related permission.

6. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases: the performance of our agreement with you, for the account, the cloud save and the leaderboard, which are the service itself; your consent, for device permissions such as location or camera, which you may withdraw at any time; and our legitimate interests in operating and securing the App — including verifying the current date so progress cannot be farmed by changing a device clock, and enforcing our rules against accounts that break them — balanced against your rights.

7. Analytics & advertising

The App does not include third-party advertising, and we do not use cross-app or cross-site tracking, advertising identifiers, or behavioural profiling. We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs that identify you.

8. This website & hosting

This companion website is served by a static hosting provider that may automatically record standard, aggregated technical logs (such as anonymised request counts, timestamps, approximate region, and error information) for reliability, security, and abuse prevention. These logs are handled by the hosting provider under its own terms, are not combined by us to identify you, and are not used for advertising. The website does not set advertising cookies.

9. Data retention

Data on your device is retained for as long as the App is installed and until you delete it (by clearing the App's data or uninstalling).

Your account and cloud save are retained for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes them, and this is not reversible — we do not keep a separate backup we could restore from. Any aggregated hosting logs are retained by the hosting provider for a limited period in line with its standard practices.

10. Data security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. On the server, access rules are enforced per-account so a signed-in account can read and write only its own save; your sign-in session is held in your device's encrypted storage. Data on your device is also protected by your device's own protections (screen lock, operating-system updates, and the permissions you grant), and the App is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for safeguarding access to your device and for keeping your password confidential.

11. Children's privacy

Solo Gainz is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in a way that concerns you, please contact us and we will address it. Onboarding asks for a date of birth and does not accept one under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and want an account removed, contact us and we will delete it and its data.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them:

  • Access & portability: your progress is visible within the App. For a copy of the data held against your account, contact us.
  • Correction: you can edit your profile and preferences within the App. To correct your date of birth or player name, contact us.
  • Deletion: clearing the App's data or uninstalling removes the local copy. To delete your account and the cloud copy, contact us at the address in section 17 and we will action it.
  • Permissions & consent: you can grant or revoke device permissions at any time in your device's system settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. If you are a California resident, we do not sell or share your personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

13. International users

Play is processed on your device wherever you are located; your account and cloud save are held by our hosting provider, which may process them outside your country under that provider's safeguards. This companion website may be delivered through servers or content-delivery networks located in other countries; by using it, you understand that any limited technical logs described above may be processed in those locations, with appropriate safeguards applied by the hosting provider.

14. Do Not Track

Because we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not need to respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals differently. We simply do not perform such tracking.
